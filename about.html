About Free CV Photo

Free CV Photo is a small website with three free tools: a CV maker with photo and personal details, a photo background remover, and a passport size photo maker.

You do not need to sign up. Your photos and CV details are processed in your own browser and are not uploaded to our servers.

We want these tools to be simple, free and easy to use on a phone. If you have an idea or find a problem, please contact us.
