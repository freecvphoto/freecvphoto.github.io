Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Short version: your photos and CV details stay on your device. We do not upload, store or see them.

What we do not collect

There is no account or sign up. Photos you choose and details you type in the CV maker are not sent to our servers.

Data saved on your device

The CV maker saves what you type in your own browser, so it is still there when you come back. This data stays on your device only. Use the “Clear all my data” button on the CV page to delete it, especially on a shared phone or computer.

Background remover and passport photo

The first time you use these tools, your browser downloads an AI model and code files from public servers (jsDelivr and Google). Your photo is processed inside your browser and is not sent to those servers. Like any website you load files from, they may see your IP address.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical data such as your IP address in its server logs. See GitHub’s own privacy statement for details.

Search and statistics

We use Google Search Console to see how this website appears in Google search. It gives us general numbers, not information about individual visitors.

Visitor counter and ads in the future

We may add a visitor counter or advertising (such as Google AdSense) later. These services can use cookies. If we add them, we will update this page and explain what they collect.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest update.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the feedback form. The form is provided by Google Forms. What you type in it, and your email if you choose to add it, is received by the site owner and handled by Google under Google’s own privacy policy.
